The surviving build was packaged September 2, 2026 as a self-contained Linux x64 Avalonia/.NET application.

- Program.cs
- App.axaml
- App.axaml.cs
- MainWindow.axaml
- MainWindow.axaml.cs
- MainWindow.Features.cs
- MainWindow.Join.cs
- MainWindow.News.cs

The exact source formatting, comments, and some high-level names may need to be reconstructed from the compiled assembly.
